How Your Protection Score Was Determined

Your Protection Score is an informational screening score based on the property information and conditions you reported. Factors may include approximate material age, time since the last reported formal inspection, and conditions you identified during the assessment.

Certain reported conditions may result in a recommendation for professional evaluation regardless of the calculated score.

Sources & Reference Information

Umbrella's screening criteria are informed by publicly available manufacturer information, published industry guidance, material characteristics, expected service-life considerations, and commonly recognized indicators of deterioration or damage.

Protection Score Disclaimer: The Umbrella Property Protection Score is an informational screening tool based on information provided by the user. It is not a professional inspection, diagnosis, engineering evaluation, structural assessment, code-compliance determination, insurance determination, or guarantee of property condition, performance, remaining service life, or value.
